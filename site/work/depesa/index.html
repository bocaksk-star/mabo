depeša: an automated Slovak news service

A Slovak news service that condenses the day’s stories into short summaries in three languages, refreshed by an automated pipeline without a newsroom.

Visit depesa.eu ↗

What was built

A Slovak news service that condenses the day's stories from home and abroad into short summaries. An automated pipeline refreshes the site through the day, so it stays current without a newsroom.

  • Seven sections, from domestic news and politics to tech and crypto
  • Slovak, English and German editions
  • Installable Android app, RSS feed and a full archive
  • Evergreen pages for name days, Bitcoin price and running topics
  • news
  • automation
  • multilingual
  • android
